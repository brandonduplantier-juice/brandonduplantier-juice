## Computational biology and longevity

- **[Epigenetic Aging Clock](https://github.com/brandonduplantier-juice/aging-clock)** ([live dashboard](https://brandon-aging-clock.streamlit.app)) - DNA methylation biological-age predictor. Predicts age to within 5.65 years on held-out blood (Pearson r 0.892) from a self-selected 176-CpG signature, and holds at 6.36 years on an independent cohort.
- **[RNA-seq Aging Differential Expression](https://github.com/brandonduplantier-juice/rnaseq-aging-de)** - Bulk RNA-seq DE on young versus old human cortex (GSE104704) with the standard DESeq2 workflow. 119 genes significant at 5% FDR.
- **[NHANES Biological Age and Mortality Risk](https://github.com/brandonduplantier-juice/nhanes-phenoage)** - Levine PhenoAge biomarker score on NHANES clinical labs linked to public mortality records, tested with Cox proportional-hazards survival models against a chronological-age baseline.
- **[Longevity Intervention Evidence Explorer](https://github.com/brandonduplantier-juice/longevity-evidence-explorer)** - Interventions graded on a transparent evidence rubric with PubMed citations. Nothing reaches the top grade, because no intervention has proven human lifespan extension.
- **[Single-Cell Aging Pathway Analysis](https://github.com/brandonduplantier-juice/single-cell-aging)** ([live dashboard](https://brandon-single-cell-aging.streamlit.app)) - Tabula Muris Senis young versus old, with pseudobulk differential expression (not per-cell pseudoreplication) and cell-type composition shifts.

## Engineering principles

- Define success before implementation and distinguish tested behavior from assumptions.
- Preserve reproducibility through versioned code, documented methods, and explicit limitations.
- Treat privacy, security, and maintainability as product requirements.
- Use AI as an engineering collaborator, not a substitute for evidence or accountability.
